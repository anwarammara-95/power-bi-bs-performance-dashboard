# Balance Sheet Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyse financial position
across equity, liabilities and assets for FY 2025 & FY 2026.

## Project Overview

This project presents a Balance Sheet Analysis Dashboard that provides a
clear overview of the financial position of the business.

The dashboard helps users analyse key financial metrics, identify the
drivers of change in the financial position of the business, and compare
yearly performance of each component of the Balance Sheet.

## Key Features

-   Working Capital KPIs
-   Variance analysis
-   Current Ratio, Debt-to-Equity Ratio and Quick Ratio comparison
-   Asset composition analysis
-   Annual performance and comparison of Balance Sheet components
-   Graphical representation of Balance Sheet performance analysis

## Business Insights

-   **Owner's Equity:** Total Equity has increased by about 80%, around
    ₹45.02 lakhs.
-   **Investments:** Investments also showed a growth of 6%, suggesting
    an increase in the amount invested.
-   **Current Liabilities:** Current Liabilities have also increased at
    a rate of 64%, leading to a fall in the Current Ratio and Quick
    Ratio. Since both are above 1, it can be ascertained that the
    liquidity of the company is healthy, but their decline suggests they
    need attention and should be investigated.
-   **Fixed Assets:** Fixed Assets fell 58%, likely due to depreciation
    or disposals. They make up 1% of Total Assets, hence the impact is
    small.
-   **Total Assets:** Total Assets increased by 42% (₹82.56 lakhs),
    coinciding with an increase in Owner's Equity (₹45.02 lakhs) and
    Current Liabilities (₹45.04 lakhs), while Loans Liabilities reduced
    (₹7.50 lakhs). The Debt-to-Equity Ratio fell by 0.63, suggesting a
    shift from debt financing to equity financing.
-   **Overall:** The business's asset base has increased and its
    Debt-to-Equity Ratio has declined. However, the sharp increase in
    Current Liabilities of 64% needs to be investigated further.

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Financial Position KPIs

Provides a high-level view of Working Capital, Current Ratio, Quick
Ratio and Debt-to-Equity Ratio.

### 2. Balance Sheet Movement

Provides the position of Assets, Liabilities and Equity for FY 2025 & FY
2026, simultaneously highlighting the variance.

### 3. Asset Composition

Highlights the distribution of assets among Fixed Assets, Investments
and Current Assets.

## Project Purpose

The objective of this project was to create an interactive and
easy-to-understand financial dashboard that transforms raw Balance Sheet
data into meaningful business insights.
